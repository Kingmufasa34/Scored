## What to listen for
- **Stuck repetition:** "question, question, question, question, question for the class". A word looping while the brain catches up.
- **Restarts mid-thought:** "But here's the thing, that I've been-But nobody told me…"
- **Losing an argument by counting:** "That's two things." "That's two, two things." Denial that proves the point.
- **"I haven't said nothing"**: the double negative as pure defence.
- **The relationship line:** "Why you got a great memory when it comes to embarrassing things that I said?" One person keeps the record, the other resents being recorded.
- **Laughter inside sentences**, not after them.
