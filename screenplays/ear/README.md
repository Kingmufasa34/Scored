# ear/

Real conversation, written down as it was actually said. Claude reads these
before drafting dialogue, to match the *texture* of real talk: the restarts,
filler, overlaps, repetition, and the way people dodge.

**What goes here:** transcripts of voice notes, recorded conversations (with
everyone's consent), verbatim interviews, things overheard and jotted down,
podcast or documentary transcripts you found striking.

**How to write them down:** exactly as said. Keep the "um"s, the false starts and
the half-sentences. Mark overlaps with `/` where the next speaker comes in, and
pauses with `(.)` for a short one or `(3s)` for a timed one. One conversation per
file, with a line at the top about the situation, e.g.
`# Two sisters in a hospital car park, arguing about who rings Dad`.

**Privacy:** change names. Claude uses these for rhythm and texture only and never
lifts real people's details into a script.
