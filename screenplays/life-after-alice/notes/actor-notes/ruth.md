# RUTH: Actor's Notes

_For whoever plays Ruth in **LIFE AFTER ALICE**. Draft for Tendai to push back on._

## One line

A kind, experienced counsellor who is still, every time, slightly out of her depth, and who's honest enough to let it show.

## What she wants

To get them talking to each other instead of at her. And to keep to time, because the next couple's in the waiting room.

## Tactics

1. **Flap a bit.** "The car park's a nightmare." Warm, flustered, chatty. She's a person, not a guru.
2. **Get out of the way.** She lets them fight. She writes things down, or pretends to.
3. **Read aloud, carefully.** "Daniel to join a cycling club." She knows this is a grenade and she's holding it gently.
4. **Ask the one good question.** The Tuesday. Slow, careful, patient. This is the only time she's really in charge.
5. **Keep to time, apologetically.** "Sorry, we're nearly at time." Awkward. She hates this bit.

## Voice signature

English, sixties. Warm, slightly flustered, lots of "um". Slower than the couple; she's the still point in the room. Never wise-sounding, never a narrator voice.

## For the voice (table read)

**Voice description (for ElevenLabs Voice Design):**
"English woman in her sixties, warm and kind counsellor, slightly flustered and chatty, natural conversational voice with gentle pauses and the odd 'um'. Not a storyteller, not theatrical."

**Baseline:** warm, conversational, a little hesitant.
**Tags she gets:** `[warmly]` `[nervous]` (opening only) `[cautiously]` `[softly]` (the Tuesday questions).
**Never:** sad, crying, dramatic.
