# THE DIRECTOR (the reader of the action): Notes

_The voice that reads scene headings, action and parentheticals in the table read. Draft for Tendai to push back on._

## One line

A good first AD reading the script at a table read: clear, steady, unsentimental, and invisible.

## How to read

- **Even.** Same energy from "Fade in" to the title card. The actors carry the emotion; the reader carries the picture.
- **Plain.** Read the action like a description of what the camera sees, not like an audiobook narrator. No drama in the voice for the vending machine; the words "He hits the glass" do the work.
- **Brisk on headings**, a beat slower on the images that matter (the satsuma peel, the highlighters, the scar).
- **Parentheticals are a murmur**: "Reading." "Into her cardigan."

## For the voice (table read)

**Voice description (for ElevenLabs Voice Design):**
"British man in his forties, calm, clear, neutral and understated, like a director reading stage directions at a table read. Even pacing, unhurried, dry, not dramatic, not a movie-trailer voice."
