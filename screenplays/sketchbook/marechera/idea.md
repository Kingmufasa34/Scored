# Marechera (parked idea)

A film about Dambudzo Marechera. Parked 26 October 2026, nothing written yet.

## The feeling

Chaos that is unsettling and never burns out, until an abrupt end.

## The thread: he is always reaching

Like *Uncut Gems*: he is always trying to snake his way out, and every move to get
out sets the next trap. He is not a victim things happen to; he sets them off.

1. **Oxford.** Reaching for acceptance. Talks, provokes, charms his way through
   every confrontation; each escape makes him more of a problem. Kicked out.
2. **The book.** Writes *The House of Hunger*, wins the prize. The win arrives on
   paper, but what he was after (respect, being a writer, not an "African writer")
   never does. The plates at the dinner. Winning and still not arriving is the
   cruellest beat, the middle of the film.
3. **The film.** Home to see the book made into a film. Sure one more rewrite, one
   more argument will save it; every fix brings it down faster. It falls apart.
4. **Abrupt end.** Cut mid-reach, maybe mid-word. No deathbed, no legacy card.

## Things worth keeping

- **No release valves.** Every scene starts in motion and leaves before it settles.
- **Pressures collide**, as in *Uncut Gems*: money, his mother, people who want
  something from him, the work, all in the same scene.
- **The stammer is the small reach.** His prose was a torrent; his speech caught.
  The big reaches (Oxford, the prize, the film) and a small one in nearly every line,
  both always snagging. People talk over him.
- **Energy never drops but the register keeps changing**: funny, frightening,
  tender for two seconds, ugly. Otherwise chaos goes numb.
- **The ending.** *Uncut Gems* kills Howard the moment his bet comes in. Ours could
  cut just as one reach finally seems to work, or the moment before we know.
