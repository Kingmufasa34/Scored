Guides

Fold & cut

  1. Print single-sided, landscape, 100% / actual size, background graphics ON.
  2. Fold the sheet in half the long way; unfold. Fold in half the short way twice to crease the 4×2 grid.
  3. Unfold flat and cut the centre slit (✂) — only across the middle two panels.
  4. Fold long-ways again, push the ends together so the slit opens to a diamond, and collapse into a booklet reading 1→8.

Generating art? Match the ratio: --ar 11:8.5 (Letter) or --ar 1.41:1 (A4), landscape.
