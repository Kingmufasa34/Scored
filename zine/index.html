Fold & cut

1. Print single-sided, landscape, Actual size / 100%, background graphics ON.
2. Fold the sheet in half the long way, unfold; fold in half the short way twice to crease the 4×2 grid.
3. Unfold flat and cut the centre slit (marked ✂) along the middle two panels only.
4. Fold long-ways again and push the ends together so the cut opens into a diamond; collapse into a booklet reading pages 1→8.

Panels → pages

Page 1 is the cover, page 8 the back. The top row prints upside-down on purpose — that's what makes the folded booklet read in order.

Generating art

Match the sheet's aspect ratio: --ar 11:8.5 (US Letter) or --ar 1.41:1 (A4), both landscape.
